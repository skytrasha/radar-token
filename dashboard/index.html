Pre-Token Radar

Every protocol in this snapshot that has no token yet, ranked by the launch value its own TVL, its own week of growth and its own age imply. Tokens that already trade are kept apart, below.

How the number is made

implied = TVL × 0.25 × (1 + clamp(Δ7d, −60, 120)% × 0.5) × (0.6 + min(age, 1095)/1095 × 0.4)

Every row goes through this one function. No protocol is named in the code, and the page recomputes each row from the snapshot's figures to show the arithmetic and the board agree.

No protocol on the board matches those filters.

Already trading: kept out of the ranking

These protocols have a token. They are shown with the market's own numbers and are never scored against the pre-token board, because a launch that already happened is not a launch to catch.
